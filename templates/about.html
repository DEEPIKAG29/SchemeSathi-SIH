Problem Context

The Problem

Marginalized and grassroots entrepreneurs (SC/ST, women, rural artisans, micro-retailers) struggle to discover and access verified government subsidies. Schemes exist across dozens of state and central ministries with fragmented portals, dense legal jargon, and opaque eligibility criteria.

Our Innovation

The SchemeSathi.ai Solution

SchemeSathi provides structured, rule-based algorithmic matching paired with automated criteria explanation. Entrepreneurs input standard demographic and business parameters and immediately receive verified schemes ranked by objective compatibility.

System Architecture Flow

Input 1. User Profile

Demographics, Category, Income, Sector, Area

Database 2. Verified Scheme DB

Central & State initiative rules dataset

↓
Engine 3. Eligibility & Weighted Scoring

Dynamic scoring across 7 categorical weights

↓
Output 4. Explainable Recommendations
