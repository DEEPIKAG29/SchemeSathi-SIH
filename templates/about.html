SMART INDIA HACKATHON 2026

Simplifying Access to Government Welfare Schemes

SchemeSathi is an intelligent digital platform built to connect micro-entrepreneurs and local artisans directly with official concessional credit without complex paperwork.

THE CHALLENGE

Information Fragmentation & Delays

India hosts over 60 million micro-enterprises, yet fewer than 15% successfully secure formal financial assistance. Due to scattered portal information and complex channel finance routing, applicants frequently face confusion, misrouted files, and high middleman exploitation.

OUR SOLUTION

Deterministic Matching & Safe Routing

We provide a rule-based scheme recommender, an advanced amortization calculator with moratorium grace periods, and a geospatial partner locator that routes users strictly to Net NPA-safe bank desks.

Rule-Based Pre-Screening

Instantly evaluates category quotas and income ceilings to guarantee absolute eligibility.

Bilingual Accessibility

Seamless non-refresh switching between English and Shuddh Hindi for complete inclusivity.

Prudential Bank Routing

Calculates real road-driving routes to active bank branches while filtering out high NPA risks.
