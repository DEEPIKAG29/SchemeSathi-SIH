HOW IT WORKS

A transparent, rule-based approach to connecting businesses with the right government support.

The Problem

Entrepreneurs and small business owners often miss out on available government subsidies and schemes due to fragmented information across multiple portals.

Our Solution

SchemeSathi matches verified eligibility criteria against an entrepreneur's specific demographics, location, and business parameters using a deterministic scoring engine.
