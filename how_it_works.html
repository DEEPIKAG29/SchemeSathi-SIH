How SchemeSathi Works

A transparent, interactive roadmap to help you discover welfare schemes, calculate concessional EMIs, and find safe bank desks instantly.

The Challenge Faced by Entrepreneurs

Small business owners and local artisans often miss out on government subsidies because information is scattered across multiple portals. Complex bureaucratic rules and the fear of loan rejections create major barriers.

Our Intelligent Solution

SchemeSathi matches verified eligibility criteria against your specific age, category, income, and business sector using a deterministic rule engine—eliminating middlemen and ensuring direct financial access.
